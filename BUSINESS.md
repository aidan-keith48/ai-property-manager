## Pricing model

- Initial setup fee: ~R7,000–R15,000 per client (repo-per-client deployment).
- Ongoing retainer: covers maintenance, improvements, and new feature requests
  per client.

Not an engineering decision and does not affect Phase 1 scope or timeline.
